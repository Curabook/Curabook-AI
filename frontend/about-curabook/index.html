Frequently Asked Questions

What is Curabook PHI and who is it for?

What happens metabolically after stopping Ozempic or Wegovy?
After stopping semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy), the body undergoes rapid metabolic changes. Ghrelin surges to 2-3x baseline within 2-4 weeks. HbA1c begins rising within 6-8 weeks. Fasting insulin rises approximately 26% as insulin sensitivity decreases. Lipid levels begin reversing toward pre-medication values. Weight regain averages 0.4 kg per month. Importantly, these changes occur before the scale shows significant movement — meaning people feel fine while their metabolic health is deteriorating. Curabook PHI tracks these markers through regular lab uploads to catch changes early. Source: West et al., BMJ 2026; Horn et al., JAMA Internal Medicine 2025
What is the GLP-1 metabolic cliff and how does Curabook detect it?
The GLP-1 metabolic cliff is the period of rapid metabolic reversal that occurs after stopping GLP-1 medications. Curabook PHI detects cliff signals by comparing sequential lab reports and flagging: HbA1c rise of 0.25% or more between readings; fasting glucose rise of 15% or more above personal baseline; fasting insulin elevation (often the earliest detectable signal); triglyceride or LDL rise of 15-20%. When any marker crosses these thresholds, PHI sends an alert and explains the clinical significance in plain language.

Which lab tests does Curabook PHI analyze?
Curabook PHI extracts and tracks: HbA1c (glycated hemoglobin), Fasting glucose, Fasting insulin, Total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol, Triglycerides, Blood pressure markers, Complete metabolic panel values including kidney and liver function, Vitamin D, Ferritin, B12, Thyroid function (TSH, T3, T4), and any other markers present in the uploaded PDF. What is the ghrelin rebound after stopping GLP-1 medications?
Ghrelin is the primary hunger hormone. GLP-1 medications suppress ghrelin as part of their appetite-reducing mechanism. When the medication stops, ghrelin levels rebound — often to 2-3x pre-medication baseline — causing intense food noise and hunger that feels overwhelming. This overshoot is biology, not willpower failure. The rebound peaks at days 14-28 post-cessation (based on medication half-life: semaglutide 7 days, tirzepatide 5 days) and gradually normalizes over 2-3 months.
