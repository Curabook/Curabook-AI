Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

HIPAA Compliant GDPR Compliant DPDP Act 2023 (India)

At Curabook AI, your privacy is a fundamental human right. We are committed to protecting your Personal Health Information (PHI) and personal data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication details (via Supabase).
  • Health Data: Medical documents (PDFs/Text) you explicitly upload.
  • Conversations: Chat logs generated during your interaction with the AI.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to:

  • Provide personalized medical context responses.
  • Maintain your chat history.
  • Improve AI accuracy (only with anonymized data).

3. HIPAA Compliance (USA)

We adhere to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. Your Protected Health Information (PHI) is encrypted at rest and in transit. We execute Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with our cloud providers where applicable.

4. GDPR Rights (Europe)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access your data.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can delete your account and all associated data permanently via the "Settings" menu.

5. DPDP Act Compliance (India)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Consent: We process data only based on the explicit consent you provide upon signup.
  • Data Fiduciary: Curabook AI acts as a Data Fiduciary responsible for your data security.
  • Grievance Redressal: For data-related grievances, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@curabook.ai.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption for database storage and TLS 1.3 for data transmission. We do not sell your personal health data to third parties.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at curabook@gmail.com
