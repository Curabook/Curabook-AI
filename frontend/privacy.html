Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

GDPR Compliant DPDP Act 2023 (India) AES-256 Encrypted

At Curabook.com, your privacy is fundamental. We are committed to protecting your personal health information and personal data. Curabook PHI is an educational wellness tool — it is not a covered entity under HIPAA, and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication details (via Supabase).
  • Health Data: Medical documents (PDFs/Text) you explicitly upload.
  • Conversations: Chat logs generated during your interaction with the AI.
  • Activity Logs: Behavioral data you choose to log (steps, sleep, food).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to:

  • Provide personalised health information responses based on your stored lab data.
  • Maintain your conversation and health history.
  • Improve AI accuracy (only with fully anonymised data — your name and ID are removed before any AI processing).

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption for database storage and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. Your name and personal identifiers are stripped before any content is sent to AI models. We do not sell your personal health data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. GDPR Rights (Europe)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time.
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Delete your account and all associated data permanently via Settings → Delete Account.
  • Portability: export all your data as JSON via Settings → Export My Data.

5. DPDP Act Compliance (India)

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Consent: We process data only with your explicit consent, provided at account creation.
  • Data Fiduciary: Curabook.com acts as the Data Fiduciary responsible for your data security.
  • Grievance Redressal: For data-related concerns, contact us at privacy@curabook.com.

6. Medical Disclaimer

Curabook PHI is an educational wellness tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or prescriptions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making any decisions about your health. PHI is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy: privacy@curabook.com
