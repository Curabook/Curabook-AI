Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

AES-256 Encrypted AI Never Sees Your Name Never Sold

Curabook is a small, independent platform built to help people manage their health after GLP-1 medication. We take your data seriously — not because a law forces us to, but because you're trusting us with something personal.

1. What We Collect

  • Email address — used to create and identify your account.
  • Lab reports and health documents — only what you choose to upload.
  • Chat history — your conversations with the AI, stored so you don't lose context.
  • Behavioral logs — steps, sleep, food entries you manually log.

We don't collect anything in the background. No tracking pixels, no ad identifiers, no device fingerprinting.

2. How We Use It

  • To give you personalised responses based on your own lab data.
  • To maintain your health history across sessions.
  • Before anything reaches an AI model, your name and personal identifiers are removed. The AI works only from anonymised health data.

We do not use your data to train AI models without your explicit opt-in.

3. How We Protect It

Your data is stored with AES-256 encryption at rest and transmitted over TLS. We use Supabase for database infrastructure, which is hosted on secured cloud servers. Access to your data is restricted — our team cannot read your health documents or chat history in plain text.

4. Your Control

  • Delete your account — removes all your data permanently. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  • Export your data — download everything as JSON from Settings → Export My Data.
  • Questions or requests — email us at privacy@curabook.com and we'll respond within 5 business days.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Curabook is an educational wellness tool — not a doctor, not a diagnostic service, not a substitute for medical advice. Everything it tells you is for informational purposes only. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your health.

6. Contact

For any privacy concerns: privacy@curabook.com
