Insurance Advocacy Guide

What is the Advocacy Brief?

PHI translates your lab data into insurance-ready language — organizing your clinical facts (BMI, HbA1c, medication history) into a structured Medical Necessity Support Packet for medications like GLP-1 agonists (Zepbound, Wegovy, Ozempic). Your doctor uses this as a reference when submitting a prior authorization.

 Common US PA Criteria for GLP-1s
  • BMI ≥ 30 (obesity) or BMI ≥ 27 with comorbidity
  • Type 2 Diabetes diagnosis (HbA1c ≥ 6.5%)
  • Documented failed diet/exercise program
  • Prior medication failures (Metformin, etc.)

How to use it

1
Upload your lab reports

PHI needs BMI, HbA1c, glucose, cholesterol, and weight data. More reports = stronger evidence packet.

2
Tell PHI your medication history

Chat: "I've been taking Metformin 500mg since 2022 with limited effect." PHI stores this and includes it in the packet.

3
Generate the packet

Click "Generate My Brief" below or ask PHI directly. PHI scores your evidence and produces a structured document.

4
Share with your provider

This is informational. Your doctor reviews it and submits the actual PA. PHI never contacts insurers directly.

⚕️ Important: This is a health information tool — not a medical or legal service. The Advocacy Brief is educational only. Your healthcare provider makes all clinical and authorization decisions.
