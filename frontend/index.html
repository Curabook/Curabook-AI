Reduced-Frequency Dosing
Extend injections from weekly to every 10-14 days. Allows the hypothalamus to gradually recalibrate while the medication remains active.

Microdosing Protocol
Fractional doses (0.2-0.6mg range) suppress food noise while minimizing GI side effects — the #2 reason for discontinuation.
